Riverside Garden open day

Twelve new beds, one long table and a seed swap. Come and see what the season grew.

WhenSaturday 14 June, 10:00 to 16:00
WhereThe gate on Mill Lane, next to the footbridge

What is on

Volunteers show the beds they look after and answer questions about growing on a small plot. The seed swap runs all day; bring what you saved last autumn.

  • Guided walks at 11:00 and 14:00
  • Compost corner: how the three bays work
  • Soup and bread at the long table from noon

Getting there

There is no parking at the garden. The 42 bus stops on Mill Lane, and the cycle racks are inside the gate.
