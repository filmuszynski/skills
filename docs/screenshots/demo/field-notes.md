# Riverside Garden: season notes

The garden opened twelve new beds this spring. These notes collect what worked, what
did not, and what the committee should decide before the autumn meeting.

## What worked

Shared watering rotas kept every bed alive through the July heat. Two volunteers per
evening was enough, and nobody had to cover more than one shift a fortnight.

The seed swap brought in forty new members. Most of them stayed for the whole season.

## What did not

The compost bays filled faster than they emptied. By August the third bay was full of
material that had not broken down, and the turning schedule fell apart during the
holidays.
